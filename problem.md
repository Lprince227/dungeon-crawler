# Problem

People who want a quick game during a short break. Many people dont have time to play a long game but still want a gaming experience. This will help people who don't often have time to play long form games or for people who like short and sweet gaming experiences that can be played in the terminal.

# Initial requirements

- R1: Let the player move through a grid-based dungeon using keyboard commands
- R2: Include enemies that the player can fight, with health and damage logic
- R3: End the game by either winning (reaching an exit) or losing (health reaches zero)
- R4: Have the ability to save the game to a file and load it again
